Baby Name Planning

Choosing a name for baby is one of the most exciting parts of getting ready, but it can also feel very daunting. Whether you already have a list of favorites or you are starting from scratch, these tips can help you narrow your choices down and feel confident in your choice.

Tips for Choosing a Name

  • Say it out loud: Try the first, middle, and last name together a few times to hear how it sounds.
  • Check the initials: Write out the full initials to make sure they do not spell out anything unexpected.
  • Consider nicknames: Think about what shortened versions or nicknames might come from the name, both the ones you love and the ones you might not.
  • Look up the meaning and origin: Many parents like choosing a name with a special meaning or a connection to family history.
  • Consider spelling: A unique spelling can make a name stand out, but it may also mean spelling it for others often.
  • Take your time: It is okay to wait until you meet baby before making your final decision!

Resources for Name Ideas

If you are looking for inspiration, BabyNames.com lets you search names by meaning, origin, and style. You can also see which names are trending with the Social Security Administration's popular baby names list, which is updated every year.
