Shopping Checklist

Getting ready to welcome baby can mean a long list of things to buy, and it is common to feel unsure about where to start. This page will help you figure out what you really need, compare some of the bigger items you may purchase, and request a personalized shopping list made just for your family.

Choosing a Sleep Space

One of the first big decisions many parents face is where baby will sleep. Pack 'n plays, bassinets, and cribs all have their own strengths, so the right choice depends on your space, your budget, and how long you would like to use it. Here is a quick comparison to help you decide.

Feature Pack 'n Play Bassinet Crib
Pros Easy for traveling, napping, and flexibility to move from room to room Easy to keep baby close to the caregiver's bed Long term arrangement and sleeping space
Cons Mattress is thin, more difficult for caregiver to reach in to grab child Baby will outgrow it quickly More expensive, not very portable
Portability Can be folded up and easily moved around Fairly lightweight and easy to move from room to room Typically kept in one space once assembled
How long it can be used Newborn through toddler years Baby's first few months Newborn through toddler years; some convert to a toddler bed
Size Medium, but can be folded up to store Small and can fit in narrow areas Large, needs a dedicated space
Price range $60 - $250 $50 - $300 $150 - $600, not counting mattress
