Hospital Bag Checklist

Packing your hospital bag ahead of time means one less thing to worry about when the big day comes. Many parents aim to have their bag packed and ready by around 36 weeks, since baby does not always wait for the due date! Keep your packed bag somewhere easy to remember and grab, like by the front door or in the car.

Every birth experience is different, so think of this list as your starting point. Check with your hospital or birth center too, since many provide some of these items for you.

For You

  • Photo ID, insurance card, and any hospital paperwork
  • A copy of your birth plan, if you have one
  • Comfortable robe or loose clothing
  • Non-slip socks or slippers
  • Nursing bras or comfortable bras
  • Toiletries, lip balm, and hair ties
  • A loose, comfortable outfit to wear home
  • Phone and an extra-long charging cord

For Baby

  • An installed rear-facing car seat (most hospitals will check to ensure it is installed correctly)
  • One or two outfits to go home in, in newborn and 0-3 month sizes
  • A swaddle or receiving blanket
  • A hat and socks or mittens

For Your Support Person

  • A change of clothes and toiletries
  • Snacks and a refillable water bottle
  • A pillow and blanket from home
  • Phone charger and headphones
  • Entertainment since labor can take awhile and have downtime

For an even more detailed list, visit the American Pregnancy Association's hospital bag packing list as another resource.
