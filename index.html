Plan for Baby with Ease

Welcome! Expecting & Prepared is a resource for soon-to-be parents getting ready to bring home a little one. Our goal is to help you plan and prepare for your family’s new addition by providing helpful information and resources. Here are some of the planning tools we’ve built for you:

  • A shopping checklist so you know exactly what to stock up on before baby arrives
  • A checklist for packing your hospital bag, to help you plan for a comfortable birthing experience
  • Resources and ideas to keep in mind when choosing baby’s name
  • Guidance from experts on caring for your newborn at home, including sleep, feeding, and illness
